Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
Core collaboration hours are Monday through Friday, from 10:00 to 16:00 local time.
Employees must remain reachable through the approved communication tools during working hours.

Equipment:
The company provides a laptop and a monitor for approved remote employees.
Additional equipment requires a written justification and manager approval.

Security:
Company documents must be accessed only through approved systems and secure networks.
