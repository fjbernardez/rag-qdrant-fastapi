Employee Handbook

Vacation policy:
Full-time employees receive 15 paid vacation days per calendar year.
Vacation days must be requested at least 7 days in advance.
Unused vacation days do not roll over automatically unless approved by Human Resources.

Sick leave:
Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year.
Medical documentation may be required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

Public holidays:
The company observes national public holidays listed in the annual internal calendar.
